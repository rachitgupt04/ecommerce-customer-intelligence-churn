## 5. Timestamp & Temporal Consistency Audit

- **`fact_orders.order_purchase_timestamp`:** 100% populated (0 nulls). Range: `2016-09-04 21:15:19` to `2018-10-17 17:30:18`.
- **Missing Delivery Dates:** 2,965 orders lack delivery customer dates. Auditing reveals these belong to unfulfilled or canceled orders (e.g. 625 canceled, 609 unavailable, 1,107 shipped).
- **Carrier System Anomaly:** 23 orders recorded a customer delivery timestamp slightly earlier than the purchase timestamp due to carrier tracking system time-zone or manual logging errors. These are logged as warnings and preserved without discarding the order entity.

## 6. Transformation Summary

1. **Category Taxonomy Resolution:** Handled 610 products with missing categories by mapping to `unknown_category` and translated all 73 Portuguese categories into English.
2. **Spatial Compression:** Reduced raw 1,000,163 geolocation records into a high-performance 19,015-row dimension table with mean coordinates and deterministic city/state resolution.
3. **Dual Identity Preservation:** Verified that all 99,441 orders link to 96,096 distinct `customer_unique_id` values, ensuring the analytical customer model can properly isolate the 2,997 repeat buyers for RFM and churn prediction.
4. **Relational Schema Loading:** All 9 tables were successfully initialized with primary keys, foreign keys, and analytical indexes.
