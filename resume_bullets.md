### Option 1: Data Scientist / Machine Learning Engineer (End-to-End ML Focus)
> **Engineered an end-to-end customer churn prediction and intelligence system** across 10,000 customers and 28,000+ transactions, achieving **0.952 ROC-AUC, 0.994 PR-AUC, and 92.9% F1-score** on an untouched holdout set by implementing cost-weighted XGBoost and Random Forest models with stratified 5-fold cross-validation. Enforced strict snapshot-based cutoff dates to eliminate look-ahead data leakage, formulated a financial cost-benefit optimization matrix that reduced expected business loss from **\$33K+ to \$7.2K** by tuning decision thresholds, and integrated **SHAP TreeExplainer** to deliver local attribution and prescriptive retention playbooks.
